# TIA and Early Electrochemical Development

These files record selected investigations that informed the final analogue-path and processing choices.

They include:

- early step-response tests with different power and shielding conditions;
- LPTIA and HSTIA comparisons in PBS/methylene-blue measurements;
- external `2 MOhm` HSTIA operation;
- blank-peak smoothing and filter-stability analyses;
- notes on current sign, endpoint extraction, and analogue settling.

The measurements were exploratory and were not all performed under the final protocol. They are included as engineering provenance, not as final NanoStat specifications. The numbered validation directories contain the results used for formal system claims.

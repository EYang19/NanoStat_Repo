# SWV voltage-profile validation

## Status

Direct NI myDAQ captures are now available for the `5 mV` full-sequencer,
`2 mV` full-sequencer and `1 mV` segmented profiles. Each profile has a
complete timing figure with positioning, plus a separate theory-versus-
measurement comparison figure over the active scan. The separate High-Z
evidence figure is stored under test 03.

The capture supports the intended relative SWV timing, staircase step height
and pulse amplitude. It is supporting waveform evidence rather than a
precision voltage calibration, because the myDAQ absolute-voltage accuracy is
larger than a single millivolt step.

## Purpose

Verify that NanoStat generates the intended SWV potential sequence and that the observed current response follows the expected dummy-cell stimulus.

## Planned protocol

Record the potential waveform at the electrode interface with an oscilloscope if the measurement setup has adequate bandwidth and common-mode range. The current fallback uses an NI myDAQ and a dummy-cell connection. Acquire the 5 mV, 2 mV and 1 mV segmented profiles and compare step height, pulse timing, scan range, point count and segment boundaries against the firmware sequencer settings.

## Report placement

Methods: SWV implementation and validation method. Results: hardware/waveform verification, before sensor results. The dummy-cell proxy is evidence of the complete excitation/readout path, but it is not a direct oscilloscope measurement of the electrode voltage.

## Required files

Place oscilloscope or myDAQ CSV/screenshots, dummy-cell CSVs, the analysis
script, and figures showing the measured/reconstructed potential profile here.
The current comparison is generated by
`code/plot_swv_profile_comparison.py`, with metrics in
`data/swv_profile_comparison_metrics.csv` and report notes in
`data/swv_profile_comparison_analysis.md`.

Generated figures:

- `figures/swv_profile_5mv_theory_vs_mydaq.png`
- `figures/swv_profile_2mv_theory_vs_mydaq.png`
- `figures/swv_profile_1mvseg_theory_vs_mydaq.png`
- `figures/swv_profile_5mv_comparison.png`
- `figures/swv_profile_2mv_comparison.png`
- `figures/swv_profile_1mvseg_comparison.png`

The first three files contain only the complete timing traces. The last three
are standalone exports of the theory-versus-myDAQ comparison panels, for a
total of six figures.

The sequence index is anchored to the first valid approximately `-450 mV`
stair, identified from its first rising edge. The standalone comparison
windows are then selected near `-400 mV` and retain time on the horizontal
axis. They show 2 steps for 5 mV, 5 steps for 2 mV, and 10 steps for 1 mV.
The 1 mV window remains inside the first segment so the segmented hold is
avoided.
The reported step/pulse error is relative. The lower-panel theory is given a
small timing alignment because myDAQ and NanoStat do not share a hardware
trigger. Signed transition edges provide the visual phase alignment, while
the plateau overlay bias/RMSE is calculated only at the centres
of the high and low plateaus, avoiding artificial error from square-wave
transitions. This separates a real absolute voltage offset from the accuracy
of the programmed increments.

The 5 mV and 2 mV traces are continuous full-sequencer scans. The segmented
1 mV trace retains the programmed staircase within each segment, but includes
two measured hold intervals of approximately `0.37 s` and `0.30 s`; these
holds are outside the standalone 1 mV comparison window and should not be
described as temporally equivalent to a continuous 1 mV sequence.

## Recommended report layout

Do not place the six raster files in a two-column grid. At half-column width,
the stair transitions and annotation become too compressed. Instead, group
the figures by profile and use three full-width figure blocks:

1. `5 mV`: complete timing trace as panel (a), focused comparison as panel (b).
2. `2 mV`: complete timing trace as panel (a), focused comparison as panel (b).
3. `1 mV segmented`: complete timing trace as panel (a), focused comparison as panel (b).

Place the two panels vertically within each block and use one caption per
profile. The regenerated files use a slightly taller paper-friendly aspect
ratio and 300 dpi, so they remain readable at approximately full text width.
